# PocketEncryptor Privacy Policy

Effective: 3 November 2026 · Applies to PocketEncryptor 2.0 and later on Android, iOS, macOS and Windows, and to the command-line tool.

**Short version: PocketEncryptor collects nothing. Your files, your password and everything about how you use the app stay on your device.**

## What the app does with your data

- **Files.** The app reads only the files you choose, and writes only where you tell it to. Encryption and decryption happen on your device. While it works, the app keeps a temporary copy in its own private storage and deletes it when the run finishes (and again at the next start, in case the app was closed mid-run).
- **Passwords.** Your password is used only to encrypt or decrypt the files in the current run. It is never saved, logged or sent anywhere, and the app wipes the key made from it from memory as soon as it's done.
- **Nothing leaves your device.** The app has no network code. It doesn't connect to the internet, has no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking, and includes no third-party SDKs that do.
- **Permissions.** The app asks for none. It reaches files only through the system's own file pickers, the Share sheet, "Open with" and drag and drop.
- **Backups.** On Android, the app opts out of cloud backup, so nothing from its private storage is copied off the device.

## Data collected

None. No personal data is collected, stored, shared or sold, and none is shared with Google, Apple, Microsoft or anyone else.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.
